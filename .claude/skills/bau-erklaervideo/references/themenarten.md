# Themenarten — the series' video types (random pick, 2026-11-24)

The user (2026-11-24): no fixed rotation any more — every video takes
**one of these types at random** (SKILL.md step 0), then the best topic
of that type. Every type keeps the house standard: **exactly 45 s**, Jan,
the realistic look, one core idea, a hook that leaves a question in the
head, the answer as a **picture** at the very end, CTA last.

Learned from viral 3D explainer shorts (Zack D. Films, 29.8 M
subscribers, 2026-11-24): one question per video, asked in the first
second; the video lives for the answer; the answer is something you
**see** (the melon falls apart, both bullets land at once); a fast,
surprising reveal makes people rewatch.

## D — Duell

- Hook = both contenders + the question, nothing else: „Abgefeuerte
  Kugel gegen fallen gelassene – welche landet zuerst?“ The viewer
  guesses in their head (that is the open loop).
- Beats: what speaks for X, what speaks for Y (each a picture), the mid
  twist makes the „obvious“ winner wobble. The answer = the race / test
  shown in slow motion at the end.
- Comment question: „Was hättest du getippt?“ style (new every time).

## E — Experiment

- „Kann man mit Zahnseide eine Melone schneiden?“ — a test people are
  curious about but would never do; 3D shows it happening.
- Safety as in format B: nothing dangerous to copy, no how-to for risky
  things, warnings stay warnings.
- The result is the money shot at the end (slow motion, a second angle).

## F — Rätsel zum Mitmachen

- Frame 0 shows the puzzle (matchsticks, a weighing riddle, a logic
  picture); the hook challenges: „Leg nur ein Streichholz um.“
- In the middle a short visual „Denkpause“ (the puzzle alone on screen,
  2–3 s, ticking sound) — the viewer tries. Then one wrong try, then the
  answer at the end, shown step by step.
- Comment question: „Hast du's in 10 Sekunden geschafft?“ style. A
  riddle with a science twist is best (the answer teaches one idea).

## G — Wahre Geschichte

- A real, documented incident with one person (a fall, a rescue, an
  accident everyone survived) — told as a mini-thriller and explained:
  why did they survive? One core idea (physics / body).
- **Facts checked with web search, real names only when public, never
  gore, never mock victims; deaths are not entertainment.** Low-poly
  people, no attempt at a real face.
- Hook: „Er fiel aus 6 Kilometern – ohne Fallschirm.“ → „Wie überlebt
  man das?!“

## H — Dein Körper verändert sich

- A habit that **visibly** changes the viewer's body over time (mouth
  breathing → face shape, screen time → posture, sitting → hips). Shown
  as a 3D before/after. Not a health tip: the change is the spectacle.
- Careful wording („kann“, „bei vielen“), never medical advice, facts
  checked.

## I — So wird's gemacht

- How a famous thing people own is made: the basketball's grip, the
  sneaker sole, the chocolate bar's snap, the pencil lead in the wood.
- The factory as the world; the trick of the process is the core idea;
  the finished thing in the viewer's hand at the end.
